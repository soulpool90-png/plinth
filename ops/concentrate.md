# Concentration playbook (Phase 4)

Triggered by monthly portfolio review when double-down fires OR month ≥ 5.

## Rules

1. Identify the winner (first to 5 paying customers, else highest MRR).
2. Move ≥80% of Daily / Growth / Weekly agent time to that product.
3. Archive losers:
   - Keep marketing/SEO pages
   - Soft-disable new free resource creation if cost is high
   - Redirect CTAs on archived product pages to the winner where relevant
4. Winner expansion checklist:
   - [ ] Annual plan emphasized on pricing
   - [ ] Upsell Team from Pro in-product
   - [ ] Features requested by ≥3 paying users
   - [ ] Retention email via Polar/Resend (only if RESEND configured)
   - [ ] Comparison pages vs the category leader
   - [ ] Integration guides (Stripe, Astro, Cursor MCP, GitHub)

## MRR math

$100k ARR ≈ $8,333 MRR ≈ 300–440 × $19–28 blended. Prefer annual upfront for cash.

## Do not

- Launch a fourth product until one of the three clears $2k MRR.
- Spend on ads.
- Spend Cloudflare upgrades until 3× covered.
